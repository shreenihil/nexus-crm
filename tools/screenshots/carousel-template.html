The Motivation

The Traditional CRM Trap: Bloat & Data Custody

Most enterprise CRMs hold tenant data hostage in proprietary silos, calculate currencies via rigid US-dollar assumptions, and slow teams down with sluggish interfaces.

💡 Sovereignty Principle: Organizations should own their encryption keys (BYOK), verify audit trails cryptographically via SHA-256 hash chains, and operate seamlessly in native ₹ INR units.
